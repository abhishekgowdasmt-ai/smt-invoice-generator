Shree Maruthi Travels

Safety, Punctuality & Comfort Since 2017

Founded with a vision to provide corporate Bangalore with reliable, premium employee transit, SMT has grown into a leading ETS operator. We focus on Proactive Operations—solving commute bottlenecks before they occur.

All SMT vehicles undergo rigorous safety checks and are fitted with active GPS telemetry tracking, first-aid safety packages, and stereos for employee comfort.

— Mr. Byre Gowda, Proprietor
